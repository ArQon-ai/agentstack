# The Agent Monetization Playbook: 7 Revenue Streams

*How to build a sustainable business around agentic systems.*

---

## The Opportunity

The agent economy is here. But how do you make money?

This issue covers 7 proven revenue streams — from consulting to products to content.

---

## Stream 1: Consulting ($150-400/hr)

**What you do:** Help companies build, optimize, and deploy agents.

**Who pays:** Startups, enterprises, agencies.

**How to start:**
1. Build a portfolio (open source, case studies)
2. Publish content (Twitter, blog, newsletter)
3. Offer free audits
4. Convert to paid engagements

**Typical deal:** $10K-50K project, 4-8 weeks.

---

## Stream 2: Technical Content & Ghostwriting ($2-5K/article)

**What you do:** Write technical content for companies.

**Who pays:** AI companies, dev tools, agencies.

**How to start:**
1. Build a portfolio (your blog, open source docs)
2. Pitch companies directly
3. Offer samples
4. Build relationships

**Typical deal:** $3K/article, 2-4 articles/month.

---

## Stream 3: Newsletter Sponsorship ($500-2K/issue)

**What you do:** Sell sponsorships in your newsletter.

**Who pays:** AI tools, dev tools, cloud providers.

**How to start:**
1. Grow to 1,000+ subscribers
2. Publish consistently
3. Create a media kit
4. Reach out to sponsors

**Typical deal:** $1K/issue, 4 issues/month.

---

## Stream 4: Open Source + Commercial ($49-499/license)

**What you do:** Build open-source tools, sell commercial licenses or support.

**Who pays:** Enterprises using your open-source project.

**How to start:**
1. Build something useful (AgentStack)
2. Open source it
3. Offer commercial features
4. Provide support contracts

**Typical deal:** $199/license or $2K/month support.

---

## Stream 5: Micro-SaaS ($29-149/month)

**What you do:** Build a small agent-powered SaaS product.

**Who pays:** Small businesses, teams, individuals.

**How to start:**
1. Identify a specific pain
2. Build an MVP in 2 weeks
3. Charge from day one
4. Iterate based on feedback

**Typical deal:** $49/month, 100 customers = $4.9K MRR.

---

## Stream 6: Digital Products ($49-199)

**What you do:** Create and sell ebooks, courses, templates.

**Who pays:** Engineers, founders, operators.

**How to start:**
1. Write a comprehensive guide (The Playbook)
2. Create video course
3. Build templates and checklists
4. Sell on Gumroad or similar

**Typical deal:** $99/product, 50 sales/month = $4.95K.

---

## Stream 7: Agency/Studio ($5K-30K/project)

**What you do:** Build custom agent solutions for clients.

**Who pays:** Enterprises, startups with budgets.

**How to start:**
1. Start with consulting
2. Build a team
3. Productize services
4. Scale with systems

**Typical deal:** $15K project, 2-3 projects/month.

---

## The Portfolio Approach

Don't rely on one stream. Build a portfolio:

| Stream | Monthly Revenue | Time Required |
