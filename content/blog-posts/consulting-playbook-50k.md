# From First Client to $50K: The Consulting Playbook

*How to build a high-value agent consulting practice.*

---

## The Foundation

Before your first client:

### Build Credibility
- Open source project with 100+ stars
- 10+ technical blog posts
- 5+ Twitter threads with engagement
- 1 newsletter with 500+ subscribers

### Define Your Niche
- "Agent engineering for fintech"
- Not "AI consultant"
- Specificity = higher rates

### Set Your Rates
- Initial: $100-150/hr
- After 3 clients: $200-250/hr
- Expert: $300-400/hr
- Never bill hourly (project-based)

---

## Finding Clients

### Channel 1: Content Marketing
```
1. Write about problems you solve
2. Share specific numbers and results
3. Include CTA: "DM me if you have this problem"
4. 1 in 50 DMs converts to a call
5. 1 in 5 calls converts to a client
```

### Channel 2: Direct Outreach
```
Target: CTOs at companies building agents
Message: "I noticed you're building X. I helped Y company solve similar problem. Worth a 15-min chat?"
Conversion: 5-10% response, 20% to call, 50% to client
```

### Channel 3: Referrals
```
Ask every client: "Who else has this problem?"
Offer: 10% referral fee or free hours
Best channel: 70% of my clients come from referrals
```

### Channel 4: Partnerships
```
Partner with:
→ AI consultancies (they need specialists)
→ Dev shops (they need AI expertise)
→ Agencies (they need technical depth)

Split: 70/30 or 60/40
```

## The Sales Process

### Step 1: Discovery Call (30 min)
```
Questions to ask:
→ What are you building?
→ What's the timeline?
→ What's the budget?
→ What have you tried?
→ What's blocking you?

Don't pitch. Listen.
```

### Step 2: Audit/Assessment (Free or $500)
```
Deliverable: 5-10 page document
→ Current state
→ Identified issues
→ Prioritized recommendations
→ Implementation plan

This builds trust.
And reveals the real scope.
```

### Step 3: Proposal
```
Structure:
→ Problem statement
→ Proposed solution
→ Scope and deliverables
→ Timeline
→ Investment
→ Next steps

Price anchor: $10K-50K for 4-8 week projects
```

### Step 4: Negotiation
```
Never discount without reducing scope.

Instead:
→ Payment plans (25% upfront, rest on milestones)
→ Phased approach (start small, expand)
→ Performance bonuses (exceed targets, get bonus)
```

## Scaling Beyond $50K

### Option 1: Raise Rates
- $150 → $300/hr
- Same hours, 2x revenue
- Requires stronger positioning

### Option 2: Productize
- Turn services into products
- Templates, frameworks, tools
- One-to-many vs one-to-one

### Option 3: Build a Team
- Hire junior consultants
- Train them
- Take 30% margin
- Scale to $200K+

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Pricing Too Low
**Fix:** Double your rate. You'll lose price-sensitive clients but gain better ones.

### 2. Scope Creep
**Fix:** Document scope. Charge for changes. Say no gracefully.

### 3. No Contract
**Fix:** Always use a contract. Even for friends. Especially for friends.

### 4. Ignoring Marketing
**Fix:** 50% of time on delivery, 50% on finding next client.

### 5. Not Specializing
**Fix:** Pick a niche. Become the expert. Charge premium.
