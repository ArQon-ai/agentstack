## Brand Voice

- **Technical but accessible** — We go deep, but we don't gatekeep
- **Fast and irreverent** — We move fast and we know it
- **Anonymous collective** — "We" not "I", no individual faces, the brand IS the entity
- **Anti-corporate** — No buzzword soup, no vague mission statements
- **Confident** — We know our stuff and we don't apologize for it

## Tone Guidelines

**DO:**
- Use contractions (we're, don't, can't)
- Write short, punchy sentences
- Use technical terms correctly and precisely
- Make bold claims and back them up
- Use humor and dry wit
- Be direct — no hedging

**DON'T:**
- Use corporate speak ("leverage", "synergy", "paradigm shift")
- Write long walls of text without breaks
- Be overly humble or apologetic
- Use AI-generated-sounding fluff
- Promise what we can't deliver

## Visual Identity

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: `#0A0A0F` (Deep void black)
- Secondary: `#6366F1` (Electric indigo)
- Accent: `#22D3EE` (Neural cyan)
- Text: `#E2E8F0` (Soft white)
- Muted: `#64748B` (Slate gray)

**Fonts:**
- Headings: Inter or Geist (clean, modern)
- Body: Inter or system-ui
- Code: JetBrains Mono or Fira Code

**Logo Concept:**
- Abstract neural network node with a pulse/wave
- Minimalist, geometric
- Works in monochrome
- Square format for social avatars
