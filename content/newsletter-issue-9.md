## Common Mistakes

### 1. Perfectionism

**Before:**
- 3 months on product
- Never launched
- $0 revenue

**After:**
- 2 weeks on MVP
- Launched immediately
- $5K first month

**Lesson:** Ship at 80%. Iterate.

### 2. Underpricing

**Before:**
- Retainers at $2K
- Projects at $5K
- Working 60+ hours

**After:**
- Retainers at $5K
- Projects at $15K
- Working 50 hours

**Lesson:** Price for value, not time.

### 3. Doing Everything

**Before:**
- Writing, coding, selling
- Admin, accounting, support
- Burned out

**After:**
- Focus on highest value
- Automate or eliminate
- VA for admin

**Lesson:** Your time is the bottleneck.
