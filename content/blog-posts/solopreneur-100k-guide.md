# The Solopreneur's Guide to Building a $100K Agent Business

*One person. One year. $100K. Here's the roadmap.*

---

## The Math

```
$100K/year = $8,333/month

Revenue mix:
→ Consulting: $4,000/month (2 clients)
→ Content: $2,000/month (ghostwriting)
→ Newsletter: $1,000/month (sponsorships)
→ Digital products: $1,000/month (playbook sales)
→ Micro-SaaS: $333/month (early stage)
```

Achievable. Proven. Realistic.

---

## Month 1-3: Foundation

### Week 1-2: Identity
- Define niche: agent engineering for [industry]
- Set up Twitter + Substack
- Create GitHub org
- Design simple brand

### Week 3-4: First Content
- Publish 4 Twitter threads
- Write 2 blog posts
- Create 1 open-source repo
- Engage with 50 people in niche

### Month 2: Consistency
- 2 threads/week
- 1 blog post/week
- 1 newsletter issue
- Daily engagement

### Month 3: Authority
- First "how I built X" thread
- First case study
- First open-source release
- 500 followers

---

## Month 4-6: Revenue

### Consulting
- Offer free 30-minute audits
- Convert 1 in 5 to paid
- First client: $2K project
- Second client: $5K project

### Content
- Pitch 5 companies for ghostwriting
- Land 1-2 deals
- $1-2K/article
- 2 articles/month

### Newsletter
- Reach 1,000 subscribers
- Create media kit
- Reach out to 10 sponsors
- Land 1-2 sponsors

---

## Month 7-9: Scale

### Consulting
- Raise rates to $200/hr
- Productize offerings
- Build templates and systems
- $5K/month

### Content
- Regular column for 1 publication
- $2-3K/article
- 2-3 articles/month
- $5K/month

### Products
- Launch playbook/course
- $99 price point
- 10 sales/month
- $1K/month

### SaaS
- Launch MVP
- 20 paying customers
- $50/month average
- $1K/month

---

## Month 10-12: Optimization

### Systems
- Document everything
- Build templates
- Automate scheduling
- Hire VA for admin

### Revenue targets:
```
Consulting: $4,000/month
Content: $2,000/month
Newsletter: $1,000/month
Products: $1,000/month
SaaS: $500/month
-------------------
Total: $8,500/month = $102K/year
```

## The Daily Schedule

```
6:00 AM: Coffee, check metrics
6:30 AM: Write Twitter thread
7:30 AM: Engage with community
8:30 AM: Client work (if any)
12:00 PM: Lunch + learn
1:00 PM: Content creation
3:00 PM: Open source / product
5:00 PM: Wrap up, plan tomorrow
```

**Key:** Consistency over intensity.

---

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Chasing Multiple Niches
**Fix:** Pick one. Dominate it. Expand later.

### 2. Perfectionism
**Fix:** Ship at 80%. Iterate publicly.

### 3. Underpricing
**Fix:** Start high. You're worth it.

### 4. No Distribution
**Fix:** 50% of time on content/distribution.

### 5. Building Before Validating
**Fix:** Sell first. Build after.
