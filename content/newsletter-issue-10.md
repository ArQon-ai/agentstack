# Building Your First SaaS: From Idea to $1K MRR in 10 Weeks

*The playbook that actually works.*

---

## The Timeline

### Week 1-2: Validation

**Goal:** Confirm people will pay

**Actions:**
- 20 customer interviews
- Ask: "Would you pay for X?"
- Ask: "How much?"
- Document pain points

**Success Criteria:**
- 10+ people say yes
- 5+ name a specific price
- Clear problem identified

**My Results:**
- 15 yes
- 10 named price
- 5 said $50+/month

**Decision:** Build

---

### Week 3-4: MVP

**Goal:** Build the minimum viable product

**Scope:**
- 1 core feature
- No integrations
- No admin panel
- Manual onboarding

**Tech Stack:**
- FastAPI (backend)
- React (frontend)
- PostgreSQL (database)
- Stripe (payments)
- Fly.io (hosting)

**Time:** 80 hours

---

### Week 5-6: Beta

**Goal:** Learn from real users

**Recruitment:**
- Newsletter subscribers
- Twitter followers
- Free for 30 days

**Process:**
- Weekly feedback calls
- Usage analytics
- Bug tracking
- Feature requests

**My Results:**
- 20 beta users
- 15 activated
- 10 weekly active
- 3 bugs found
- 2 feature requests

**Key Learning:** Users wanted simpler onboarding

---

### Week 7: Launch

**Goal:** Get first paying customers

**Channels:**
1. Product Hunt
2. Twitter thread
3. Newsletter blast
4. Hacker News
5. Reddit

**Results:**
- 150 signups in week 1
- 15 paid conversions (10%)
- $435 MRR

**What Worked:**
- Product Hunt: 40% of traffic
- Twitter: 30% of traffic
- Newsletter: 20% of traffic

---

### Week 8-10: Iterate

**Goal:** Reach $1K MRR

**Focus Areas:**
1. Onboarding (activation ↑ 30%)
2. Pricing (added $29 tier)
3. Features (top 2 requests)
4. Retention (weekly emails)

**Weekly Progress:**
- Week 8: $600 MRR
- Week 9: $800 MRR
- Week 10: $1,050 MRR

**$1K MRR achieved.**

## Lessons Learned

### 1. Validate First

**Before:** I built products no one wanted.
**After:** 20 interviews saved months.

**Rule:** No code until 10 people say they'll pay.

### 2. Scope Small

**Before:** 10 features, never launched.
**After:** 1 feature, launched in 2 weeks.

**Rule:** Solve 1 problem perfectly.

### 3. Beta is Gold

**Before:** Launched to crickets.
**After:** 20 beta users found real issues.

**Rule:** Real users > perfect product.

### 4. Launch Early

**Before:** Waited for perfection.
**After:** Launched at 80%.

**Rule:** Perfect is the enemy of shipped.

### 5. Iterate Fast

**Before:** Monthly updates.
**After:** Weekly improvements.

**Rule:** Compound improvements.
