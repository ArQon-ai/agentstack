# The Consulting Deal Playbook: How to Close $10K+ Projects

*The exact process from first contact to signed contract.*

---

## The Funnel

```
Content → Discovery Call → Audit → Proposal → Negotiation → Contract → Delivery
   1000       50             10        5          3           2          2
```

Conversion rates:
- Content to call: 5%
- Call to audit: 20%
- Audit to proposal: 50%
- Proposal to contract: 60%

---

## Step 1: Content (The Top of Funnel)

Your content does the selling.

**What works:**
- "How I solved X" threads
- Cost breakdowns
- Architecture deep-dives
- Failure postmortems

**What doesn't:**
- Generic advice
- News commentary
- Product pitches

**Goal:** Attract people with the exact problem you solve.

---

## Step 2: Discovery Call (30 minutes)

**Before the call:**
- Research their company
- Check their tech stack
- Look at their job postings
- Review their recent news

**During the call:**
```
1. "Tell me about what you're building" (5 min)
2. "What's the timeline?" (5 min)
3. "What have you tried?" (5 min)
4. "What's blocking you?" (5 min)
5. "What does success look like?" (5 min)
6. "What's the budget range?" (5 min)
```

**Key:** Listen 80%, talk 20%.

---

## Step 3: The Audit ($500-1,000)

**Why charge for audit:**
- Filters serious prospects
- Builds trust through delivery
- Reveals real scope
- Converts at 50% vs 10% for free

**Deliverable:**
- 5-10 page document
- Current state analysis
- Identified issues
- Prioritized recommendations
- Implementation roadmap

**Timeline:** 2-3 days

---

## Step 4: The Proposal

**Structure:**
```
1. Problem Statement (1 page)
   - What they told you
   - Business impact

2. Proposed Solution (2 pages)
   - Approach
   - Deliverables
   - Timeline

3. Investment (1 page)
   - Total cost
   - Payment terms
   - Milestones

4. About Us (1 page)
   - Relevant experience
   - Case studies
   - Testimonials

5. Next Steps (1 page)
   - Signature
   - Start date
   - Contact info
```

**Pricing:**
- Small project: $5-10K
- Medium project: $10-25K
- Large project: $25-50K
- Enterprise: $50K+

---

## Step 5: Negotiation

**Common objections:**

"Too expensive"
→ Break into phases
→ Offer payment plan
→ Show ROI

"Need to think about it"
→ Set deadline
→ Offer limited scope
→ Provide references

"Comparing vendors"
→ Differentiate on expertise
→ Offer guarantee
→ Provide case study

**Never discount without reducing scope.**

---

## Step 6: The Contract

**Must include:**
- Scope of work
- Deliverables
- Timeline
- Payment terms
- IP ownership
- Confidentiality
- Termination clause

**Payment terms:**
- 50% upfront
- 25% at midpoint
- 25% on delivery

**Or:**
- Monthly retainer
- Auto-billing
- Net 15

---

## Step 7: Delivery

**Week 1:** Discovery + Planning
**Week 2-3:** Implementation
**Week 4:** Testing + Hardening
**Week 5:** Documentation + Handoff

**Key:** Over-communicate. Under-promise. Over-deliver.
